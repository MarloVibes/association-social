# Franchise Mobile x NBA 2K Response Playbook

Use this guide after a Take-Two, 2K, or Visual Concepts contact accepts a connection or replies. Do not attach internal strategy documents, source code, credentials, formulas, raw datasets, or repository links.

## Approved First Attachment

Send only `Franchise_Mobile_x_NBA_2K_One_Page_Teaser.pdf` after the recipient expresses interest or asks for more information.

The teaser is a non-confidential overview. The full deck, protected demo, technical appendix, and diligence materials come later.

## When The Connection Is Accepted

Hi [Name], thank you for connecting. Franchise Mobile is a working companion-platform concept designed to make NBA 2K MyNBA and MyGM leagues persistent, social, and manageable between console sessions, while NBA 2K remains the authoritative gameplay experience.

I would appreciate a brief conversation or an introduction to the appropriate NBA 2K product, Visual Concepts, or strategic partnerships contact. I can share a short non-confidential overview if helpful.

## When They Ask For More Information

Thank you for the interest. I have attached a one-page non-confidential overview of the product concept and proposed NBA 2K integration loop.

The working prototype demonstrates mobile league operations, front-office management, commissioner tools, social coordination, CPU continuity, schedules, trades, strategy, results, and statistics. I would welcome a short product discussion to determine whether the concept is relevant to the NBA 2K roadmap.

Attach only the approved one-page teaser.

## When They Refer You To Someone Else

Thank you, I appreciate the direction. May I mention that you referred me when I contact [Name]? I will keep the initial outreach brief and non-confidential.

## When They Request A Meeting

Thank you. I would be glad to walk through the working prototype and the proposed console/mobile product loop.

For the first discussion, I suggest 20-30 minutes covering the product gap, working proof, value to NBA 2K, proposed integration boundaries, and the focused request for a product and technical evaluation.

Please let me know the preferred time, meeting platform, and attendees so I can tailor the walkthrough appropriately.

## When They Ask For The Demo

Absolutely. I can provide a protected, read-only demonstration account with seeded sample data. Please send the preferred business email address and the names of anyone else who requires access.

Demo access is issued to named recipients, is isolated from production, and can be time-limited. Do not send credentials in the same message as the demo link.

## When They Ask Technical Questions

The prototype demonstrates the product workflow but does not assume access to a public NBA 2K API. The proposed architecture would require a 2K-controlled service contract, authenticated account linking, scoped league permissions, event-based synchronization, and server-authoritative conflict resolution.

I can discuss system boundaries and integration requirements at a high level in the first meeting. Source code, proprietary simulation logic, raw player methodology, credentials, and detailed architecture are reserved for an approved diligence process.

## When They Ask Whether It Is Licensed Or Official

Franchise Mobile is an independent working prototype and proposed integration concept. It is not currently affiliated with, endorsed by, or integrated into NBA 2K, 2K, Take-Two, Visual Concepts, the NBA, or the players associations.

The purpose of the discussion is to evaluate whether an official product relationship or integration should be explored.

## When They Ask For An NDA

I am open to reviewing the appropriate confidentiality process. For the first conversation, I can keep the presentation non-confidential and focused on product value. Any source access, proprietary formulas, raw datasets, or detailed implementation material would require an agreed protected diligence process.

Do not sign or send a legal agreement without attorney review.

## Follow-Up After Five Business Days

Hi [Name], I wanted to follow up on my NBA 2K connected-league companion concept. The working prototype focuses on keeping MyNBA/MyGM leagues active through mobile management, social coordination, and console game-result continuity.

I would value a brief conversation or direction to the appropriate product or partnerships contact. Thank you for your time.

Send one follow-up. Do not repeatedly message the same person.

## Meeting Readiness Checklist

- Confirm the recipient's name, title, company, and relevance.
- Tailor the opening to product, development, publishing, or corporate strategy.
- Use the isolated demo project, never production.
- Verify founder and viewer walkthroughs before the meeting.
- Keep the one-page teaser and pitch deck free of passwords and private links.
- Prepare a 10-minute guided demonstration and a 5-minute question buffer.
- State clearly what is working today and what is a proposed integration.
- Ask for a product and technical evaluation, not immediate source access.
- Record who received materials and when access should expire.
