# Franchise Mobile x NBA 2K

## A Connected League Companion Concept

Franchise Mobile is a working product concept for making NBA 2K MyNBA and MyGM leagues persistent, social, and manageable between console sessions. NBA 2K on console or PC remains the authoritative gameplay experience.

## The Opportunity

Online franchise communities coordinate schedules, trades, rules, league decisions, and conversation across disconnected chats and spreadsheets. Activity slows when members are away from the console or a franchise becomes vacant.

Franchise Mobile gives the same league a persistent mobile home.

## Proposed Connected Loop

Franchise Mobile <-> 2K account and online services <-> MyNBA/MyGM league <-> Console or PC gameplay

Managers prepare and coordinate on mobile. Scheduled games are played or resolved through NBA 2K. Results, box scores, standings, injuries, awards, and progression return to the shared league.

This is a proposed architecture for evaluation and does not assume that a public NBA 2K integration API currently exists.

## What The Working Prototype Demonstrates

- Persistent 30-team league operations and schedules.
- GM Lounge, league chat, moderation, voting, and notifications.
- Trade rooms, trade block, commissioner review, and CPU-controlled vacancies.
- Player evaluation, scouting, tiers, archetypes, grades, and development.
- Matchup strategy, coaching preparation, results, box scores, and statistics.
- Standings, awards, offseason management, and continuous league activity.

## Strategic Value

- Extends MyNBA/MyGM engagement beyond active console sessions.
- Brings league coordination into the NBA 2K ecosystem.
- Helps private and public leagues remain organized and active.
- Creates more reasons for users to return to NBA 2K for scheduled games.
- Establishes a community foundation that could later expand into MyCAREER teammates, squads, events, and organized competition.

## Initial Request

A private product and technical evaluation with NBA 2K product leadership, Visual Concepts MyNBA/MyGM development leadership, and the appropriate 2K online, mobile, or strategic partnerships representatives.

## Status

Independent working prototype and proposed integration concept. Not currently affiliated with, endorsed by, or integrated into NBA 2K, 2K, Take-Two, Visual Concepts, the NBA, or the players associations.
